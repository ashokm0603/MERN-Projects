# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
## Project 16: Good Deeds Tracker (Social Impact)

### 1. Project Description
The Good Deeds Tracker is a social impact platform that encourages and celebrates kindness in everyday life. Users log acts of kindness they have performed or witnessed, follow friends' charitable activities, and earn community recognition badges for consistent positive contributions. The platform gamifies altruism to motivate individuals to perform more intentional acts of goodness and build a culture of kindness within communities.

### 2. Core Scope
The application focuses on deed logging, social sharing, gamification through badges and leaderboards, community following, and impact visualization. It is designed to be uplifting and positive, requiring careful moderation to maintain authenticity and prevent abuse of the reward system.

### 3. Functionalities (Minimum 25)

#### Deed Logging
1. **Good Deed Submission:** Users log a deed by providing a title, description, category, and optional photo evidence.
2. **Deed Category Tags:** Classify deeds by type (e.g., Environmental, Helping a Stranger, Volunteering, Donation, Emotional Support, Random Act of Kindness).
3. **Beneficiary Tagging:** Optionally specify who benefited (e.g., an individual, a community, an animal, the environment).
4. **Time & Location Logging:** Record the date, time, and optional location of the deed for personal reflection and mapping.
5. **Recurring Deed Templates:** Save frequently performed good deeds as quick-log templates for efficient re-logging.

#### Social Feed & Community
6. **Public Activity Feed:** A chronological feed showing deeds shared publicly by followed users and community highlights.
7. **User Profiles:** Public profiles showing a user's deed history, earned badges, impact statistics, and follower counts.
8. **Follow System:** Follow friends, community leaders, or inspiring users to see their good deeds in the personal feed.
9. **Like & Appreciation Reactions:** React to friends' deeds with positive reactions (e.g., Heart, Inspired, Amazed) instead of generic likes.
10. **Comment on Deeds:** Leave encouraging comments or personal stories on others' deed posts.
11. **Deed Sharing:** Share a deed card to external social media (Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp) with a beautifully formatted visual card.

#### Gamification & Rewards
12. **Badge System:** Earn badges for reaching milestones (e.g., "First Deed," "10 Deeds," "Volunteer Champion," "Eco Warrior," "Daily Giver" for 7-day streak).
13. **Points System:** Earn points per logged deed, with bonus points for deeds with photo evidence or high community appreciation.
14. **Community Leaderboard:** A weekly and all-time leaderboard showing top contributors by points, deeds logged, or specific categories.
15. **Streak Tracker:** Track consecutive days of logging at least one good deed to encourage daily habits.
16. **Level Progression:** Progress through levels (e.g., Seed → Sprout → Tree → Forest) as points accumulate.

#### Impact Visualization
17. **Personal Impact Dashboard:** Visual summary of total deeds performed, categories covered, badges earned, and impact timeline.
18. **Category Breakdown Chart:** Pie chart or bar graph showing the distribution of deed types for self-reflection.
19. **Community Impact Map:** A map visualization showing where good deeds have been performed across the community.
20. **Monthly Impact Report:** An auto-generated monthly summary of the user's kindness activity sent via email.

#### Challenges & Events
21. **Community Challenges:** Platform-wide kindness challenges (e.g., "30 Days of Gratitude," "World Environment Day") that users can join and track.
22. **Friend Challenges:** Invite a friend to compete in a kindness challenge over a set period.
23. **Verified NGO Partnerships:** Partner with registered NGOs who can post volunteer opportunities directly within the app.

#### Admin & Moderation
24. **Deed Verification System:** Community flagging system to report fake or misleading deed logs; admins review flagged content.
25. **Admin Analytics Dashboard:** View platform-wide stats on total deeds, most active users, popular categories, and badge distribution.
