# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
## Project 5: Mental Health Check-In Journal (AI-Powered)

### 1. Project Description
The Mental Health Check-In Journal is a compassionate, AI-powered daily journaling platform designed to support users' emotional well-being. Users log their daily mood, write free-form journal entries, and receive AI-generated pattern insights, trend visualizations, and personalized coping strategy suggestions. The platform provides a private, stigma-free space for self-reflection while helping users recognize emotional patterns over time.

### 2. Core Scope
The application centers on daily mood logging, journaling, AI-based sentiment analysis, pattern recognition, and coping resource recommendations. It prioritizes user privacy, empathetic UX design, and actionable mental health insights without replacing professional therapy.

### 3. Functionalities (Minimum 25)

#### Daily Check-In & Mood Logging
1. **Daily Mood Logger:** Users select from a range of mood states (e.g., Happy, Sad, Anxious, Calm, Angry) with customizable granularity (1–10 scale).
2. **Emotion Tags:** Tag specific emotions (e.g., Grateful, Overwhelmed, Hopeful, Lonely) to provide more nuanced context beyond a single mood rating.
3. **Energy Level Tracker:** Log daily energy levels alongside mood to help correlate fatigue with emotional states.
4. **Check-In Streaks:** Track consecutive days of logging and reward consistency with streak badges.
5. **Quick Check-In Mode:** A 30-second minimal check-in option for days when users have little time for full journaling.

#### Journaling Features
6. **Free-Form Journal Entry:** A distraction-free rich-text editor for writing daily reflections, thoughts, and events.
7. **Guided Prompts:** AI-generated daily journaling prompts tailored to the user's recent mood trends (e.g., "What made you smile today?" or "What's one thing you could let go of?").
8. **Gratitude Log:** A dedicated section for users to list 1–3 things they are grateful for each day.
9. **Entry Categorization:** Tag entries by theme (e.g., Work, Relationships, Health, Personal Growth).
10. **Private Entry Locking:** Option to mark specific entries as extra-private with an additional PIN lock.

#### AI-Powered Insights
11. **Sentiment Analysis:** AI analyzes the emotional tone of journal entries and correlates it with the logged mood score.
12. **Mood Pattern Recognition:** Identify recurring patterns, such as consistently lower moods on Mondays or after social events.
13. **Trigger Identification:** AI highlights frequently co-occurring topics or tags when mood dips below a threshold.
14. **Weekly Mood Summary:** Automatically generated narrative summary of the week's emotional journey and key highlights.
15. **Personalized Coping Suggestions:** Based on identified patterns, AI recommends relevant coping strategies (breathing exercises, journaling techniques, physical activity prompts).

#### Data Visualization & Analytics
16. **Mood Trend Chart:** An interactive line chart showing mood fluctuations over the past 7, 30, and 90 days.
17. **Emotion Frequency Map:** A heatmap or word cloud visualizing the most frequently logged emotions over a period.
18. **Calendar Heatmap:** A GitHub-style calendar heatmap showing mood intensity for each day of the year at a glance.
19. **Correlation Dashboard:** Visualize correlations between mood and factors like sleep, exercise, or social activity (if tracked).

#### Wellness Resources
20. **Crisis Resource Integration:** Automatically surface national mental health hotline information if keywords suggesting distress (e.g., "hopeless," "can't go on") are detected.
21. **Guided Breathing Exercises:** An interactive, animated breathing exercise tool accessible directly from the app.
22. **Curated Resource Library:** A library of articles, videos, and exercises categorized by emotional state (Anxiety, Grief, Stress, etc.).

#### User Account & Privacy
23. **User Authentication:** Secure account creation with email/password; no social login to preserve anonymity if desired.
24. **Data Export:** Allow users to download their complete journal history and mood data as a PDF or CSV file.
25. **Account Deletion:** Permanently and completely delete all user data upon request, with a confirmation step.
26. **Reminder Notifications:** Configurable daily push/email reminders to complete the check-in at a preferred time.
27. **Customizable Themes:** Offer calming color themes and dark mode to create a comfortable journaling environment.
